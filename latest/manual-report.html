  • Observed: Harness asserted reward_popups == 1 and reward_popup_text contains "+20 coins!" — both green. Game log shows `[CHEST REWARD] Created popup for 20 coins at (-5.684, 1.5, -5.213)` — i.e. exactly above the tower.
